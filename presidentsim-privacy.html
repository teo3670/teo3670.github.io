PresidentSim Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information PresidentSim (the "app", formerly FinanceSim) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. PresidentSim is a single-player strategy game for Android published by TD Codes ("I", "me").

Information I collect

I do not run user accounts and I do not ask you for your name, email address, or any other personal details. I do not operate a server that stores your gameplay.

  • Game progress and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Advertising and device data may be collected automatically by Google AdMob, the advertising service used in the app (see below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob and Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details, app usage and ad interactions, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is handled by Google under its own policies and is not shared with me in a form that identifies you.

  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

How information is used

Information collected through advertising is used by Google to show and measure ads and to keep the app free. I do not sell your personal information.

Children

PresidentSim is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will help resolve it.

Data retention and deletion

Because gameplay data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies.

Security

I do not store your data on my own servers, which limits what can be exposed. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
