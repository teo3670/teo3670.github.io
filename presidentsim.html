The game

A political strategy game where you run a country month by month: split the tax burden, win your budget in parliament, manage an ageing and emigrating population, and build airports, ports and dams in pixel art. Take over one of ten real crises, from Weimar 1923 to Greece 2015, or design your own nation, and read your verdict in The Daily Ledger. Formerly FinanceSim.
