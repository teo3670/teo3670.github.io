About

I study Management Science & Technology at the Athens University of Economics and Business, but most of what I've actually built has happened outside the curriculum: freelance design work, a data-visualization page followed for its clarity, a couple of app ideas I keep pushing forward, and now a research role in the department's Business Analytics Lab.

What connects all of it is a preference for building my own thing over following someone else's brief — figuring out a problem, shaping it into something visual or usable, and shipping it.
