Dionysos Youth Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Dionysos Youth (the "app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is developed and operated by Theodoros Drachtidis, TD Codes ("I", "me"), for the Youth Council of Dionysos Municipality. It is intended for people aged 17 to 28.

Information I collect

Unlike a game, this app needs an account to work. I collect only what the features below require.

  • Account details you give at sign-up: your name, email address, the community you live in and your year of birth. Your password is handled by Google's sign-in service and is never visible to me.
  • Whether your email is verified.
  • Your credits: your balance, the total you have earned, and a history of each time you earned or spent credits.
  • Event attendance: which events you were checked in to, when, and which organizer scanned your card, or, if you could not show your card (for example with a flat battery), that the organizer found you by name, email or member code instead. If you tap "I'm coming" on an event, that is stored too until you cancel it.
  • Credits given by hand: if an administrator gives you credits outside an event, the amount, the reason they wrote (which you see in your history) and which administrator gave them.
  • Invitations: if you join with a friend's invite code, which member invited you, your name as it appears to them, whether you have attended your first event, and when the welcome credits were given. If you invite others, the same is stored for each person who used your code.
  • Redemptions: which offers you redeemed, when, and the one-time code that was issued. At partner shops that scan redemptions, also when the shop confirmed it and which staff account did so, and, if the cashier types them, the amount of the bill and of the discount. If a redemption is not confirmed in time, the refund of its credits is recorded too.
  • Poll votes: which option you chose in each poll you answered.
  • Event feedback: if you rate an event you attended, the rating (1 to 5 stars) and, if you write one, your comment. The comment is stored without your name.
  • Donations and transfers: credits you give to a community goal, and credits you send to or receive from another member (the amount, the date and the other member).
  • Proposals: the text of any proposal you post in "Civic Voice", and which proposals you upvoted or reported.
  • Partner-shop accounts: people who work at a partner shop use a separate shop account, created with an invite code from the Youth Council (owners) or from the shop's owner (cashiers). For these accounts I store only the email address, the person's name, the shop, the role there (owner or cashier) and which invite was used. Shop accounts have no wallet, Youth Card, year of birth or community. If the Youth Council instead links a member account to a shop, the shop and the role are stored with it.
  • Notification address: if you allow notifications, a push token that lets the app reach your phone, and whether it is an Android or iOS device.

The app does not show ads, does not use advertising or analytics trackers, does not read your contacts or location, and I do not ask for identity documents.

Camera

The camera is used only to read the QR code on a Youth Card: by organizers at the entrance of an event, by you if you choose to send credits to another member, by staff at a partner shop's till to read the code of a redemption, and by you to scan a partner shop's QR code and see its offers. Nothing is photographed, recorded or uploaded. You can use the rest of the app without granting camera access.

Notifications

With your permission, the app notifies you when a new event is added to the programme. The notification contains only the event's title, date and place. You can turn notifications off with the switch at the top of the schedule or in your phone's settings; the push token is then deleted, and it is also deleted when you sign out.

If you tap "I'm coming" on an event, the app also schedules two reminders on your phone, a day and an hour before it starts. They are created by your phone itself: nothing is sent to me or to any service, and they are removed when you cancel, sign out or delete your account.

How information is used

  • To create your account and keep it secure.
  • To show your Youth Card, award credits when you attend an event, and let you redeem them. If you cannot show your card at the entrance, to let an organizer find you and check you in by hand.
  • To give welcome credits to you and to the friend who invited you, once you have attended your first event, and to limit how many invitations can be rewarded.
  • To help the Youth Council plan its events (how many people intend to come, how many attended).
  • To count poll results and upvotes on proposals.
  • To show the Youth Council how its events were rated, so it can improve them.
  • At partner shops: to tell the cashier which discount you are entitled to, to make sure each redemption is used once, and to return your credits automatically if it is not used. Each shop sees totals for its own redemptions (how many, and the bills and discounts typed at its till), and administrators see the same totals for every shop. These statistics never show who redeemed.
  • To show which of the municipality's seven communities has earned the most credits. This ranking uses only each member's community and credits earned, never names.
  • To let administrators see overall statistics about the programme, such as the number of members per community and age group, attendance per event and redemptions per offer. These are totals only; no individual member is shown.
  • To send you a notification about new events, if you allowed it.

I do not sell your personal information and I do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • You can see your own profile, balance, history, RSVPs and votes in the app.
  • Organizers of the Youth Council see your name when they scan your card, and can see your profile details (name, email, community, year of birth) and which events you attended or said you would attend. To check in members who cannot show their card, they can also search members by name, email or member code, a few results at a time; the results show name, email, community and balance.
  • Administrators of the app can additionally see your credit history in order to resolve problems.
  • Other members see your name and community in two cases: when they scan your Youth Card (for example to send you credits), and on any proposal you post, which is published under your name for all members to read. A member who sends you credits, or receives credits from you, sees your name in their history. They never see your email, year of birth or balance.
  • A friend who invited you sees your name and whether you have attended your first event (and so whether you both received the welcome credits). They never see your email, year of birth, balance or history.
  • Upvotes are counted but not attributed: other members and organizers cannot see who upvoted a proposal.
  • Poll votes are not shown to other members, organizers or administrators inside the app. Only the totals per option are displayed. As the operator of the database I am technically able to access the stored votes, and I do not do so except to fix a fault.
  • Partner shops: when the staff of a partner shop scan or type the code of your redemption, they see your name and the offer you redeemed, so they can give you the discount. They never see your email, year of birth, balance or history. At shops that do not scan, you only show the code on your own screen. Scanning a shop's QR code or opening its page sends nothing about you to the shop.
  • Owners of partner shops see the name and role of the people with access to their shop's account. Administrators see the same for every shop.
  • Event ratings and comments are shown to organizers and administrators only as totals and as comments without names. As with poll votes, I am technically able to see who gave which rating, and I do not do so except to fix a fault.

Where information is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which acts as a service provider and processes the data on my behalf under Google's terms. Information about how Google handles data in Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and Google's or Apple's notification services, which receive only your push token and the text of the notification. If you add your Youth Card to Google Wallet or Apple Wallet, the card carries your name, community, member number and the QR code of the app's card. For Google Wallet the card is created with Google, under Google's terms. For Apple Wallet the card file is signed by an automated job and stored in your own record, which only you can read; it reaches Apple only when you tap "Add" and it is saved on your iPhone. Nothing is added to a wallet unless you ask for it, and you can remove the card from the wallet at any time.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you may ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw your consent at any time. You can change your name and community in the app. For anything else, write to me at the address below and I will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account in the app (Wallet tab → "Διαγραφή λογαριασμού") or by emailing me from the address you registered with; see how to delete your account. Your profile and sign-in are deleted at once, and your credit history, attendance records, RSVPs, votes, event ratings, redemptions, proposals, invitations, partner-shop account and push token within one hour (within 30 days for requests by email). Anonymous poll, upvote and rating totals remain, as do comments on events (which carry no name), as do the entries in other members' histories for credits you exchanged with them. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.

Shop accounts are deleted the same way, from the Shop tab → "Διαγραφή λογαριασμού": the sign-in and the access to the shop are removed. An owner can also remove a cashier's access at any time. Redemptions confirmed at the shop stay in the shop's totals without names.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 17 to 28 and asks for your year of birth at sign-up for that reason. It is not directed at anyone under 17. If you believe a younger person has registered, contact me and I will remove the account.

Security

I protect your data with technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, as the GDPR requires:

  • All traffic between the app and the database is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and Google encrypts the stored data.
  • Every read and write is checked by rules on Google's servers, whatever the app sends: members can read only their own records, and credits can change only through a verified event check-in, redemption, refund, poll vote, donation, transfer, invitation reward or an administrator's award with a written reason (never to themselves). Partner-shop staff can confirm only their own shop's redemptions, each once.
  • The app collects only what its features need, and has no ads or trackers.

If a personal-data breach ever happens that puts your rights at risk, I will notify the Hellenic Data Protection Authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and inform you without undue delay, as Articles 33 and 34 of the GDPR require. No system connected to the internet can be made completely immune to attack, which is why these safeguards and this commitment exist.
