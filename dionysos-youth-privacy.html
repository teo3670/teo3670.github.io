Dionysos Youth Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Dionysos Youth (the "app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is developed and operated by Theodoros Drachtidis, TD Codes ("I", "me"), for the Youth Council of Dionysos Municipality. It is intended for people aged 17 to 28.

Information I collect

Unlike a game, this app needs an account to work. I collect only what the features below require.

  • Account details you give at sign-up: your name, email address, the community you live in and your year of birth. Your password is handled by Google's sign-in service and is never visible to me.
  • Whether your email is verified.
  • Your credits: your balance, the total you have earned, and a history of each time you earned or spent credits.
  • Event attendance: which events you were checked in to, when, and which organizer scanned your card. If you tap "I'm coming" on an event, that is stored too until you cancel it.
  • Redemptions: which offers you redeemed, when, and the one-time code that was issued.
  • Poll votes: which option you chose in each poll you answered.
  • Donations and transfers: credits you give to a community goal, and credits you send to or receive from another member (the amount, the date and the other member).
  • Proposals: the text of any proposal you post in "Civic Voice", and which proposals you upvoted or reported.

The app does not show ads, does not use advertising or analytics trackers, does not read your contacts or location, and I do not ask for identity documents.

Camera

The camera is used only to read the QR code on a Youth Card: by organizers at the entrance of an event, and by you if you choose to send credits to another member. Nothing is photographed, recorded or uploaded. You can use the rest of the app without granting camera access.

How information is used

  • To create your account and keep it secure.
  • To show your Youth Card, award credits when you attend an event, and let you redeem them.
  • To help the Youth Council plan its events (how many people intend to come, how many attended).
  • To count poll results and upvotes on proposals.
  • To show which of the municipality's seven communities has earned the most credits. This ranking uses only each member's community and credits earned, never names.

I do not sell your personal information and I do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • You can see your own profile, balance, history, RSVPs and votes in the app.
  • Organizers of the Youth Council see your name when they scan your card, and can see your profile details (name, email, community, year of birth) and which events you attended or said you would attend.
  • Administrators of the app can additionally see your credit history in order to resolve problems.
  • Other members see your name and community in two cases: when they scan your Youth Card (for example to send you credits), and on any proposal you post, which is published under your name for all members to read. A member who sends you credits, or receives credits from you, sees your name in their history. They never see your email, year of birth or balance.
  • Upvotes are counted but not attributed: other members and organizers cannot see who upvoted a proposal.
  • Poll votes are not shown to other members, organizers or administrators inside the app. Only the totals per option are displayed. As the operator of the database I am technically able to access the stored votes, and I do not do so except to fix a fault.
  • Partner shops receive no personal data from the app. At the till you show a one-time code on your own screen.

Where information is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which acts as a service provider and processes the data on my behalf under Google's terms. Information about how Google handles data in Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you may ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw your consent at any time. You can change your name and community in the app. For anything else, write to me at the address below and I will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. To delete your account and the data linked to it, email me from the address you registered with. I will delete your profile, credit history, attendance records, RSVPs, votes and proposals; anonymous poll and upvote totals remain, as do the entries in other members' histories for credits you exchanged with them. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 17 to 28 and asks for your year of birth at sign-up for that reason. It is not directed at anyone under 17. If you believe a younger person has registered, contact me and I will remove the account.

Security

Access to the data is restricted by rules on the database: members can read only their own records, and credits can change only through a verified event check-in, redemption or poll vote. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
