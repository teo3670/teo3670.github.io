Delete your Dionysos Youth account

Dionysos Youth is the app of the Youth Council of Dionysos Municipality, developed by Theodoros Drachtidis (TD Codes). You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  • Open the app and sign in.
  • On the Πορτοφόλι (Wallet) tab, scroll to the bottom and tap Διαγραφή λογαριασμού (Delete account).
  • Enter your password and tap Οριστική διαγραφή (Delete permanently).

You are signed out straight away. Your profile and sign-in are deleted at once, and the rest of your data within one hour.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email theodoredraht@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Dionysos Youth account deletion". I will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile: name, email address, community and year of birth.
  • Your credit balance and credit history.
  • Your event check-ins and RSVPs, poll votes, offer redemptions and redemption codes.
  • Your proposals in "Civic Voice", and your upvotes and reports.
  • Your entry in the community leaderboard.

Credits left in your account are lost when it is deleted.

What is kept

  • Anonymous totals: the number of votes for each poll option and the upvote count of other members' proposals. They do not identify you.
  • Entries in other members' credit histories for credits you sent to them or received from them. These show your name as it was at the time and belong to the other member's history.

Nothing else is kept. See the privacy policy for details.
