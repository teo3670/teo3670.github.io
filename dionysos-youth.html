Dionysos Youth

Dionysos Youth Council · Android · In development

The app

A civic-engagement app for the Youth Council of Dionysos Municipality, built for residents aged 17 to 28 across the municipality's seven communities. Members carry a digital Youth Card, get checked in at the Council's events to earn Dionysos Credits, and spend them on discounts at local shops.

Screens shown with sample data.

How it works

  • Youth Card. Every member has a personal QR code. An organizer scans it at the entrance of an event and the credits land in the member's wallet.
  • Events. Upcoming actions of the Council, with a one-tap "I'm coming" so organizers can plan for the turnout.
  • Offers. Local businesses offer discounts that members unlock with their credits and show at the till.
  • Polls. Quick questions from the Council, with a small reward for taking part.
  • Civic Voice. Members post proposals for the municipality and upvote each other's ideas.
  • Community goals. Members pool credits toward shared projects, and the seven communities compete on a leaderboard.
  • Tiers. Bronze, Silver and Gold, based on the credits a member has earned by taking part.
